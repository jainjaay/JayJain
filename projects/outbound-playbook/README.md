# 📦 B2B Outbound Campaign & Objection Handling Playbook

**Target Market:** Mid-Market Logistics & Supply Chain Companies in Ontario  
**Goal:** Generate qualified discovery meetings with VP of Sales and Directors of Logistics  
**Tools Demonstrated:** Cold Email Sequences, LinkedIn Social Selling Scripts, Objection-Handling Matrix, CRM Pipeline Progression  

---

## 🎯 Target Persona & ICP Breakdown

* **Target Title:** Director of Operations, VP of Logistics, Supply Chain Manager
* **Company Size:** 50–250 Employees (Mid-Market)
* **Core Pain Points:** High freight turnover, lack of shipment visibility, manual vendor management, unoptimized logistics software integrations

---

## 📧 Cold Email Cadence (3-Touch Sequence)

### Touch 1: The Cold Outreach Email
* **Subject:** Streamlining shipment visibility for [Company Name]

> Hi [First Name],
>
> Noticed that [Company Name] has been expanding logistics routes across Southern Ontario. 
>
> Many logistics leaders I speak with in Ontario are facing delays due to manual vendor coordination and fragmented tracking systems.
>
> We recently helped an Ontario-based distributor reduce shipment tracking overhead by 25% while maintaining a 98% on-time fulfillment rate.
>
> Would you be open to a brief 10-minute chat this Thursday at 10:00 AM to see if this aligns with your team's Q3 efficiency targets?
>
> Best regards,  
> **Jay Jain**  
> B2B Sales & Outbound Prospecting  
> Waterloo, ON

## 🛡️ Cold Call Objection Handling Matrix

| Prospect Objection | Underlying Concern | Recommended Response Script |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"We already have a solution for this."** | Vendor Displacement Risk | *"Makes complete sense, [First Name]. Most teams we partner with were already using established tools. We actually don't ask you to replace your existing setup — we integrate directly to eliminate blind spots. Worth a 5-minute look to see how we compare?"* |
| **"Send me an email."** | Brush-Off | *"Happy to do that! To make sure I send over something relevant, are you currently focused more on carrier cost reduction or real-time shipment visibility?"* |
| **"We don't have budget right now."** | Timing / Value Disconnect | *"Understood. I'm not asking you to buy anything today. I'd just like to share a few benchmarks from other Ontario distributors so you have them when budget planning comes up later this year."* |
